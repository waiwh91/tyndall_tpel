TPEL – Tyndall Power Electronics Laboratory

The Tyndall Power Electronics Laboratory is a research group at Tyndall National Institute, University College Cork, led by Senior Researcher Dr Yi Dou.

The group investigates scientific and engineering problems in power electronics, including power conversion, wide-bandgap semiconductor applications, integrated magnetics, digital control and electrified energy systems. Its work connects device- and component-level research with converter and system design.
