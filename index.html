Integrated Power Electronics Team

We advance integrated power electronic systems from semiconductor devices and passive components to packaging, modelling and application-led system design.

Our aim is direct: zero-loss, zero-volume power electronics. We create knowledge, develop researchers and translate technologies towards industrial use.
