Research

The laboratory addresses fundamental and application-oriented questions in electrical energy conversion. Research is organised across components, converters and systems, with analytical work supported by modelling and experimental verification.

Particular attention is given to the interaction between electrical, magnetic and thermal design. Device characteristics, passive components, packaging, control and operating conditions are considered together rather than as independent stages.

Research areas

01

Power conversion

Converter topologies, modulation, component selection and experimental validation.

02

Wide-bandgap power semiconductor applications

Application of GaN and SiC devices in high-frequency and high-efficiency conversion.

03

Integrated magnetics and packaging

Co-design of magnetic components, interconnects, thermal paths and converter structures.

04

Digital control and energy systems

Modelling and control of power converters for electrification and energy applications.
