Research

Integrated power electronics from components to systems.

Our research combines advanced semiconductor devices and passive components with packaging, integration and predictive design. Mathematical modelling and virtual and hardware prototyping connect fundamental work to system-level demonstrators.

Research thrusts

01

Advanced devices & components

WBG and UWBG power-switch characterisation, advanced control and PMIC optimisation, novel magnetics and advanced capacitors.

02

Advanced system integration

Low-parasitic packaging, electro-thermal design, thermal management and co-integration of devices, passives and cooling structures.

03

Advanced modelling & design

Physics-based, mathematical and data-driven modelling, multi-objective optimisation and virtual prototyping.

Application roadmap

AI data-centre power deliveryHigh-current, fast-transient voltage regulation from rack-level conversion to processor point-of-load.

Co-packaged silicon photonicsCompact, low-noise power conversion integrated alongside electronic and photonic chiplets.

Edge AI hardwareMiniaturised, energy-efficient power management for distributed sensing and computing.

Aerospace electrical systemsLightweight, high-density and high-reliability conversion for harsh operating environments.

Biomedical electronicsMiniaturised, low-noise and safe power delivery for wearable and implantable systems.

Industrial electrificationHigh-efficiency conversion and integrated power modules for automation and distributed energy systems.

Research projects

FAMES Pilot Line

Integrated magnetics, high-density capacitors and PMIC integration for advanced FD-SOI and 3D power delivery.

Rinn Semiconductors

Heterogeneous integration, chiplet architectures and advanced packaging from materials to system-level applications.
